# Surface Brief: Homepage

## Scope & visitor mode
Persuade — a first-time car owner lands here, must understand what the shop is, trust it, and route themselves to their part within seconds.

## Audience, job, action, proof
Individual car owners (non-trade) searching for specific car parts. Job: find a part quickly and get to checkout. Primary action: either search by part name/OEM number, or select their vehicle and browse compatible parts. Proof: the catalog depth — if the categories and thumbnails cover their car's needs, the shop is credible.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A utility-first parts catalog that earns authority through information density, not decoration. Refuses the generic e-commerce hero + promotional card grid; every pixel serves part discovery.

OWN-WORLD: White (#fff) ground, dark forest green (#1b5e20) as the single accent and brand color, black (#111) body type. System sans-serif (Inter sourced from Google Fonts as the single upgrade over browser defaults). No gradients, no shadows beyond 1px borders, no rounded corners on layout elements (4px only on form inputs). Part thumbnails: grayscale photos on white background, label below. Category headings in green, body in black.

STORY: User lands → sees search bar immediately → either types a part name/OEM number and goes straight to results, OR selects Year/Make/Model/Engine from the vehicle bar and browses filtered categories. The left rail gives them a full catalog map at all times. They pick a category section, scan thumbnails, click a part, done.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header band (utility top bar thin strip → logo left / search center / ORDER+ORDER HISTORY right). Vehicle selector bar below (gray #f5f5f5 band, "Shop by Vehicle" label + Year/Make/Model/Engine dropdowns + "Find Parts →" button). Green announcement strip. Then immediately: two-column body — left nav (BROWSE CATALOG list, 180px) + right content (category sections with green h3 headers and thumbnail rows). Primary action is the search bar at top center.

FORM: McMaster-Carr canon executed at full fidelity for auto parts. Ordered 1st of all candidates evaluated. Approved comp: .impeccable/mocks/decision/homepage-comp-1.jpg.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
